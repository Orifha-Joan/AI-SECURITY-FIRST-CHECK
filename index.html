Scoring methodology and limitations

Calculation

The overall score is the sum of six control scores. Each control receives 0, 1, or 2 points, for a maximum of 12.

  • 0: Established and supported by evidence.
  • 1: Partially addressed or unverified.
  • 2: Absent or ineffective.

Partial and unverified controls share one point in this prototype, but the report labels them differently. An unverified control must be investigated before anyone concludes it is effective or absent.

The score is a prioritisation aid, not a validated security rating. It does not measure every possible AI risk, and it does not replace a detailed assessment, expert review, legal advice, or applicable compliance obligations.
